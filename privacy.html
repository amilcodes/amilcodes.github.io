Privacy

I use Umami to understand which projects people visit and which links and demonstrations they open.

What is measured

Page visits, referring websites, browser and operating-system categories, language, approximate location, project views, link clicks, recording opens, and broad scrolling and active-time milestones.

Query strings, URL fragments, referral paths, entered text, exact cursor positions, and screen recordings are excluded. Autoplaying media is not counted as a person choosing to watch it. I do not connect these visits to names, email addresses, social profiles, or advertising identities.

How it works

The tracker uses no analytics cookies. Umami processes IP addresses and browser information to derive approximate location and rotating pseudonymous visit identifiers. These are not a guarantee of anonymity. IP addresses are not fields in its standard analytics records; Umami and hosting providers may process or log them to operate their services. See Umami’s data definitions and privacy policy.

Your choice

This site skips analytics when your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. You can also turn it off here. This saves only an opt-out preference in this browser, not a visitor identifier. The privacy page itself does not load analytics.
